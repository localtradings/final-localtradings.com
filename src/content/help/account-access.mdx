## Account flow

Public routes preview the product. Signed-in routes under `/app/*` contain the
actual learning, quiz, and backtesting experiences.

## What to expect

- Use **email and password** for v1 auth.
- Product route protection activates when Supabase environment values are set.
- Profile data stays minimal and focused on account basics.
