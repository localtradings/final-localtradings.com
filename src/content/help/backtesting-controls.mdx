## Replay

Replay lets you step through price action instead of staring at the whole move
at once.

## Plan

Use the tool area to define direction, entry, stop loss, target, and thesis
before reviewing the outcome.

## Session rules

Saved sessions keep the replay window, trade state, thesis, and review notes so
you can return later or from another signed-in browser.

If the same candle touches both stop and target, the v1 engine resolves that as
the stop first.

Pending limit and stop orders can also fill and resolve on the same candle. The
tool is built for structured replay and review, not tick-accurate execution.

## Review

The goal is to review **process quality**, not to pretend one result proves the
system is perfect.
