## Public vs signed-in

The public site explains the product and previews the structure. Once signed in,
the `/app/*` routes become the real workspace.

## Launch scope

- Learn
- Quiz
- Backtesting

Community, wallet, notifications, and standalone settings routes are not part
of v1.
