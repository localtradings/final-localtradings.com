## Body and wick

The **body** shows the distance between open and close. The **wicks** show where
price explored outside that range before the candle closed.

## What to observe

- Is the body strong or small?
- Did price reject one side aggressively?
- Where did the candle close relative to its range?

## No pattern worship

One candle is context, not certainty. Treat it as evidence, not a command.
