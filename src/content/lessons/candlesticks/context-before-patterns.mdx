## Start with location

Ask where the candle appears:

- At support or resistance?
- Inside a range?
- After a sharp move?

## Then classify behavior

Once location is clear, decide whether the candle suggests rejection,
continuation, or indecision.

> Context changes meaning. The same candle can say different things in different locations.
