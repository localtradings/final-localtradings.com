## Rejection

Large wicks can suggest one side was pushed back. That matters more when the
candle appears at a meaningful level or after an extended move.

## Continuation

A candle closing strongly in the direction of travel can show continued
participation. The question is not whether the candle looks impressive. The
question is whether the surrounding structure supports the move.

## Review prompt

Write down what would make you trust continuation and what would make you doubt it.
