## Risk first

If you do not know how much you can lose on one trade, you are not planning the
trade clearly enough.

## Core idea

Risk per trade is a cap on account exposure. It keeps one mistake from doing too
much damage.

## Reflection

Before every setup, ask whether your size matches the risk you intended.
