## Stops are invalidation

A stop loss should define what proves the trade idea wrong. It should not be
placed randomly just to make the position larger.

## Expectancy

Expectancy asks whether the process makes sense across repeated trades. One win
or loss does not tell the whole story.

## Better review question

Did the plan make sense, and did you follow it?
