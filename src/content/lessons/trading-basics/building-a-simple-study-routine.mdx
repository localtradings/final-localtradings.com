## Keep the routine short

A study routine does not need to be complicated. It should help you do the same
useful work consistently.

## Suggested sequence

1. Review one lesson.
2. Take a short quiz.
3. Write one or two notes.
4. Save backtesting practice for when you understand the topic you are testing.

## What to avoid

Avoid copying random setups before you know what the lesson was trying to teach.
