## Price is participation

Price moves because buyers and sellers interact. A chart is not a prediction
machine. It is a record of decisions made in the market.

## Useful beginner mindset

Instead of searching for magic signals, ask:

- Who seems in control right now?
- Where did price hesitate?
- What would change my view?

## Keep it simple

You do not need advanced order flow tools to start learning this idea. Clear
notes and repetition are enough for v1.
