## Why sessions matter

Different sessions can change **participation**, **volatility**, and the pace of
price movement. You do not need to memorize everything at once. Start by
observing when activity expands or slows down.

## A beginner-first approach

- Track one or two instruments.
- Note when the session opens.
- Compare calm periods with high-participation periods.

## What to review

Ask whether your current routine tells you **when the market is active** and
whether you are trying to trade when nothing important is happening.
